Recursive proofs of Keccak-256 hash chains, fast, with Lean-certified soundness.

Design a hash-based proof system, prove in Lean that it is knowledge-sound and complete, and ship an x86-64 assembly prover. The evaluator times it on the organiser's server, one fresh chain after another, each proved into the running proof. Two tracks rank the same evaluation: the sum of prover time and a verifier charge, and the product of prover time and a verifier factor. Read the rules.

Each track has its own record and leaderboard, from the same evaluations.
