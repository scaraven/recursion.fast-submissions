# A8: final editorial and technical review of the report

Scope: `report/recursion-fast-blueprint.pdf` (168 pp., build of 2026-10-11 14:38) and `report/sections/*.tex`.
Ground truth used: `design/DESIGN.md` (v0.2 body plus §15.1–§15.4), `contract-v03/RESULT.md` (including §R3),
`submissions/baseline-v03/RESULT.md`, and `recursion-fast-idea-prompt.md`. The report was not edited.

Method:
- read every section source in full;
- compared each appendix listing line by line with `contract-v03/RecursionFast/Contract/*.lean` (script; only
  `Cost.lean` differs);
- grepped for the staleness keywords given in the brief;
- mapped figures and tables to pages, and rendered pp. 7, 22, 66, 102 and 134;
- read the build log of `build-orch/`.

Severity: **major** means a reader will take away a wrong fact or decision, or one of the brief's asks is
poorly answered. **minor** means a local inaccuracy, a layout problem or a style problem.

The main finding is that the core sections (§6–§12) were updated to v0.3 and to the round-3 fixes, but
the periphery was not:
- the exec-summary numbers;
- §4 lessons;
- §9 sketch;
- §13 some rows;
- §14 the register;
- Appendix A, Cost.lean only;
- the glossary;
- Appendix C.

That periphery still describes the v0.2 contract, the pre-migration baseline or the pre-P2d pricing.
