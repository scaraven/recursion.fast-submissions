## 7. Deviations, and what A2′ changes

1. **The brief's levers were replaced** by the two policies (Summary): `phiRS`'s alignment had already
   been fixed by S-E11, and "align only groups above `2^12`" would not have touched the 383/30–54-row
   skeleton blocks. The stride policy plus the cut keep every block at ≤ 2 aligned pieces.
2. **Modules beyond the brief's list**: `FRVIRTplExt`, `FRVIRTplBuild`, `FRVIRTplPlan` (L-circ's;
   no parallel track owns them). `FRNodeRows4Plan` was **not** changed (no instance-stride evaluator
   was needed: a split instance is two aligned pieces).
3. **3.x's plan also changes**: `TEval.planOf` (the closed `Impl.nodePlan` built at `Solution`'s start)
   uses the same `mkLay`/`alignOf`/`compMSX`, so 3.x's layouts follow the policies too (its proofs
   are generic). Its V was not measured (it is not on the 4.x verify path: `implN4F` uses
   `evalMT4`).
4. **For A2′ (it had not landed):**
   - its `posOpsG` ends in `shrN` (no `assertEq`); before B0 an open block was dropped when the
     next opened, so every position would have gone sparse (an estimate, not measured: ≈ 35 k rows at
     ≈ 0.55 µs a sparse row, ≈ 20 ms per prototype). B0's `segsGo`/
     `compMSX` close a block at the next aligned op, so the positions stay blocks.
   - `alignOf`'s position prediction `4s + 10` over-estimates after A2′ (no `assertEq` rows);
     that is safe (a block shorter than predicted pads less; a longer one becomes 2–3 pieces).
   - its `capChkOps` (worktree, `FRVIRFinals4`) puts the cap check in the finals as skeleton ops: ≈
     10 rows per entry × 1,920 + ≈ 10 + 5·`nb` per read × 867 ≈ **+55–65 k sparse rows** per
     prototype, where PACK-4.4.1 §6.1 budgets 40 k of cap-check templates. The prototypes will then
     be ≈ 690 k / 680 k; the region totals (which `FRNodeRows4Model` checks) move by the difference
     to 40 k (≈ +20 k per region, N8 ≈ 7.78 M, 1.03×). And ≈ 60 k sparse rows would cost ≈ 30 ms per
     prototype at ≈ 0.55 µs a row (`piececost`: 9.4 ms for prototype 0's ≈ 17 k sparse rows; an
     estimate) unless they are templated (PACK's plan). **Re-measure B0's rows and V
     after A2′ lands** (`nodedump4`, `ab3.sh`); B0's policies need no change for it.
