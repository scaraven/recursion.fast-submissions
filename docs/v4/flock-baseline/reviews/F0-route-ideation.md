## Q4 Security level

errorCap is per fresh query [F]. In GF(2^128) one degree-2 sumcheck round costs 2/2^128 = 2^-127 and the univariate-skip round 126/2^128 ≈ 2^-121 — **Flock's field cannot meet 2^-128 even in unique decoding** [E from App. A]. GF(2^192) (spartan-chain's E, leanVM's F192) gives 2^-185 for the skip and, in list decoding, OOD binding L²m/|F| ≈ 2^-175 at L≈70 [E], so Johnson-regime Ligerito/WHIR is admissible at 128 bits if its MCA theorem is formalised (V3-5 allows proved theorems). Queries at rate ½: ~261 LD / ~309 UD per level vs Flock's 218 [E]; proof ≈ 1.3× queries × 1.5× field bytes → Flock's 386 KiB → ~550–750 KiB [E; Flock's own 120-bit UD variant: 558 KiB [M]]. Prover: extension arithmetic ~2× (F192 vs F128) in zerocheck/PCS, ~85% of Flock's time [M Table 5] → ~0.5–0.7× throughput [E]. Grinding: admitted by `CompleteWithError` (89·2^g attempts for δ=2^-128 [F v3 §5.4]); a leading-zero test on a fresh answer is an ordinary potential event. O25-M4's "code the submitter does not author" was a mitigation for the public-oracle recognition channel, which v4 closes by key secrecy (C §1) [P]; nothing extra is needed, but say so in the entry.
